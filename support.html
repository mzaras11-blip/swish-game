Getting started

On first launch you pick a unique username — that is your identity in the game. You can play right away against the computer, and connecting a Google or Apple account is optional; it simply protects your username if you change phones.

Playing with friends

Add friends by their exact username in the Friends tab. In Split Screen, one player creates a room and shares the room code; the other joins with the code, and you watch each other's board and letter rack live as you play. Split Screen — Online Matchmaking on the Home screen pairs you with another player who is searching at the same time.

Ads and Remove Ads

S.W.I.S.H is free and supported by ads: a banner on menu screens, a short ad after finished games, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for bonuses. Ads never appear in the middle of a turn. The Remove Ads purchase in the Shop turns off banners and the after-game ads permanently.

Report a problem

If something misbehaves, email mzaras11@gmail.com with your username, your device model, and what happened — screenshots help.
