Privacy Policy — S.W.I.S.H – Crossword Battle

Effective October 19, 2026

S.W.I.S.H – Crossword Battle ("S.W.I.S.H", "the app") is a turn-based crossword battle game developed by Michael Aras. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Questions: mzaras11@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Account information. Every player picks a unique username. If you choose to connect an account, we receive your email address from Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple, linked to your username so your progress follows you to a new phone. If you play as a guest, your account is an anonymous ID with no email attached.

Game data. To run multiplayer features — friend lists, the leaderboard, and live Split Screen games — the app stores your username, scores, game results, friends, and the moves of online games in progress. This data is stored with Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication).

Advertising data. The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and information about ad views and interactions to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

On-device data. Your progress, coins, power-ups, and settings are also stored locally on your device.

How we use information

We use the information above only to operate the game: signing you in, saving your progress, matching you with friends and opponents, running the leaderboard, and showing ads that support the app. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

Data is processed by Google Firebase (accounts, game data, and notifications) and Google AdMob (advertising) strictly to provide those services. We do not share your information with anyone else except when required by law.

Your choices

You can play as a guest without providing an email address. You can limit ad tracking in your iOS settings. To request deletion of your account and its stored game data, email mzaras11@gmail.com from the address connected to your account (or tell us your username) and we will delete it.

Children

S.W.I.S.H is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
