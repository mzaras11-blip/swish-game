How to play

Your runner takes off on his own — your job is steering:

  • Swipe or tap the left/right side of the screen to switch lanes.
  • Swipe up to jump.
  • Swipe down to slide under barriers — you can slide in mid-air, too.
  • Run through the floating letter tiles in order to spell the word shown in the tray at the bottom of the screen. Grab stars along the way, and finish missions for extra targets.
  • Bump a car or a barrier and the run ends — tap PLAY AGAIN to go again.

Good to know

  • There is no account and no sign-in — just open the game and tap PLAY.
  • The game works fully offline. Your best score is saved on your device only.
  • There are no ads and no in-app purchases in SWISH CITY.
  • Sound starts when you tap — look for "Tap for sound" on the menu if the game is quiet.
