Privacy Policy — SWISH CITY

Effective: October 28, 2026

SWISH CITY is an endless-runner spelling game from A&M Bros LLC. This policy is short because the game is simple: SWISH CITY does not collect any personal information.

No data collected

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. It does not ask for your name, email, location, or any other personal information, and it contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

No internet connection

The game runs entirely on your device. It loads from the app itself and makes no network requests, so nothing about you or your play is sent to us or to anyone else.

On-device game data

Your best score and settings (such as sound on/off) are stored only on your device, inside the app. They never leave your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

SWISH CITY is a family-friendly game, and it collects no data from anyone — including children.

Changes

If a future version of SWISH CITY ever changes what data it handles, this policy will be updated and posted on this page before that version is released.
